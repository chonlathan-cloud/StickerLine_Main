# Design System: Manee-son AI Sticker (Cute Modern Edition)

## 1. Visual Identity & Vibe
- **Core Concept:** "Friendly Assistant" — Clean, soft, and extremely intuitive.
- **Personality:** Sweet, Modern, Accessible, and Calm.
- **Visual Style:** "Soft-Minimalism". Using pastel tones, large "Squircle" (rounded square) shapes, and smooth transitions. Everything should look "touchable" and simple.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary Color:** `#FF80AB` (Soft Pink) or `#9575CD` (Soft Lavender). Let's go with **Soft Lavender** for a modern AI feel.
- **Secondary Color:** `#E0F2F1` (Mint Whisper) - For success states and soft backgrounds.
- **Background:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) with very soft `#F3F4F6` sections.
- **Accent/Coins:** `#FFB74D` (Soft Peach/Gold) - For a warm, non-aggressive money feel.

## 3. Typography
- **Primary Font:** 'Quicksand' or 'Kanit' (Rounded).
- **Style:** Medium weights, avoiding heavy bold text to keep it light and airy.

## 4. Component Style (The "Easy" Specs)
- **Buttons:** - **Shape:** "Squircle" (Radius: `16px`).
  - **Style:** Soft flat design with a very subtle "inner glow" or light border.
  - **Height:** 50px.
- **Cards:** Very soft shadows (almost invisible) or just a light grey background instead of borders.
- **Icons:** Rounded, "Chunky" icons (thicker lines) in a single color.

## 5. Core Workflow UI: The 4x4 Grid
- **Sticker Cells:** Soft rounded corners (`12px`).
- **The "Keep" State:** A soft Lavender glow around the sticker and a small "Smiling Star" or "Heart" icon to indicate it's saved.
- **The "Regenerate" State:** Unselected stickers become slightly blurry or semi-transparent (`0.4` opacity).

## 6. Payment & Download
- **Interface:** Use large, easy-to-read cards. No complex tables. 
- **Buttons:** Large, clear labels like "Get 7 Coins" or "Save My Art".
