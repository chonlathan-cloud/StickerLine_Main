# Design System: Manee-son AI Sticker (Soft Bubble 3D Edition)

## 1. Visual Identity & Vibe
- **Core Concept:** "The Playful Toy" — Feels like a highly polished, friendly mobile game. 
- **Personality:** Bouncy, Cheerful, Gamified, and Extremely Intuitive.
- **Visual Style:** "Claymorphism" or Soft 3D. Elements should look tactile, chunky, and inviting to tap.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary:** `#3B82F6` (Bright Sky Blue) - Very friendly and energetic.
- **Secondary/Coins:** `#FBBF24` (Warm Sunny Yellow) - Pops well against blue and white.
- **Background:** `#F8FAFC` (Very light blue-gray) to make the white 3D cards stand out.
- **Success:** `#34D399` (Minty Green).

## 3. Component Style (The "Tactile" Specs)
- **Buttons:** - **Shape:** Completely rounded (Pill shape) and thick (`56px` height).
  - **3D Effect:** Add a thick bottom border (e.g., `border-bottom: 4px solid #2563EB`) to simulate a physical, pressable button. No heavy drop shadows, just solid layered colors.
- **Cards & Grid:** White backgrounds, thick rounded corners (`20px`), and a subtle internal drop shadow to make them look like soft trays.
- **Typography:** 'Nunito' or 'Varela Round' — Chunky, bold, and highly legible.
