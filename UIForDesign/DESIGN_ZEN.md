# Design System: Manee-son AI Sticker (Warm Zen Edition)

## 1. Visual Identity & Vibe
- **Core Concept:** "The Calm Studio" — A stress-free, organic, and highly comforting environment.
- **Personality:** Warm, Polite, Minimalist, and Organic.
- **Visual Style:** Japanese minimalism (Muji style). Warm earthy tones, ample breathing room, and absolute simplicity.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Background:** `#FAF9F6` (Off-white / Warm Cream) - Eliminates the harshness of pure white screen glare.
- **Primary Text & Accents:** `#4A4036` (Deep Warm Brown/Mocha) instead of harsh black.
- **Primary Action:** `#E07A5F` (Muted Terracotta / Soft Orange-Red) - Warm and inviting.
- **Secondary/Coins:** `#E9C46A` (Soft Sand Gold).

## 3. Component Style (The "Organic" Specs)
- **Buttons:** - **Shape:** Gently rounded corners (`12px`).
  - **Style:** Completely flat. No shadows, no gradients. Pure solid colors. 
- **Cards:** No borders. Just use a slightly different shade of cream (`#F2EFE9`) to separate sections.
- **Typography:** 'Prompt' or 'Inter' with medium weight. Text should look soft and highly readable, avoiding pure black.
- **The 4x4 Grid:** Simple, clean layout. Selected stickers have a warm `#E07A5F` outline.
